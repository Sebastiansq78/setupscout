About SetupScout

Made for people who love setups.

Our mission

SetupScout helps gamers discover equipment, compare options and find inspiration for a desk that fits their needs and budget. We aim to make choosing gaming gear easier, whether you are starting out or upgrading.

How our links work

Our product buttons currently lead to Amazon search results. They are not affiliate tracking links, and we do not claim a commission from these links. We may add affiliate links in the future and will disclose them clearly if we do.

Need to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are the prices on SetupScout live?

No. The current site directs you to Amazon to check current prices, availability and shipping. We do not currently have a live price feed.

Can I buy products directly on SetupScout?

No. Product links take you to Amazon, where you can check out with the retailer.

Does SetupScout earn commission?

Not through the current ordinary Amazon search links. If we add affiliate tracking links in the future, we will disclose that clearly.

How do I choose the right gaming gear?

Think about your budget, the games you play, comfort, compatibility and which upgrade will make the biggest difference to you.

Privacy

Privacy information

SetupScout currently has no account creation, contact form or custom analytics in this file. Product buttons lead to Amazon, which has its own privacy practices when you visit its website. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information as part of providing the service.
